Sports brought in the most, $5,314.21, followed by Sci-Fi and Animation. The top five are within 18% of each other, so no single category dominates. Revenue here is the sum of Payment amounts, joined through Rental and Inventory to each film's Category.

Validated SQL DuckDB dialect · read-only · LIMIT applied 
SELECT c.name AS category, SUM(p.amount) AS revenue
FROM payment p
JOIN rental r        ON r.rental_id = p.rental_id
JOIN inventory i     ON i.inventory_id = r.inventory_id
JOIN film_category fc ON fc.film_id = i.film_id
JOIN category c      ON c.category_id = fc.category_id
GROUP BY c.name
ORDER BY revenue DESC
LIMIT 5;
